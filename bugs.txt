RoverSim known bugs
Things that work differently from the real rover, or just wrong.
If you find a new one, tell an officer and we'll add it here.

- distanceAhead() on the real rover returns 99 when nothing is in range.
  The simulator always sees the edge of the map, so it never returns 99.
- forward(0) and backward(0) do nothing on the real rover, but the
  simulator stops with an error.
